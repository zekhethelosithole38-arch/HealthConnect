# HealthConnect Clinic – Appointment Analytics

## Project Overview
This project is part of the **AnalystLab Africa Internship (8 weeks)**.  
We are working with the HealthConnect Clinic dataset (5,000 records, 18 variables) to explore appointment attendance and no‑show patterns.  

**Central Question:**  
How can HealthConnect Clinic use data to reduce missed appointments and improve the patient support experience?

## Progress (Week 4)
- Reviewed dataset structure and quality.  
- Identified missing values (notably reminder channel ~27%).  
- Highlighted key variables: appointment type, previous no‑shows, reminder channel, distance, waiting time.  
- Defined initial business questions and KPIs.  
- Outlined cleaning and analysis approach.

## Week 5
### Are reminders helpful in improving attendance rates?  
- Yes. 73% of patients received reminders (mostly via SMS), and attendance was higher among those reminded.

### Are certain appointment periods associated with higher no‑show rates?  
- No significant differences by day or time of appointment. Attendance outcomes were evenly distributed, showing that scheduling period is not a major driver.

### Do previous no‑show patterns help identify patients at greater risk of missing future appointments?  
- Yes. Patients with a history of missed appointments were more likely to no‑show again, making past behavior a strong predictor.

### Key Insights
- Overall no‑show rate: 49% vs. attendance rate: 46%.
- Distance to clinic strongly influences attendance — no‑show rates rise with distance.
- Seniors (65+) are the most reliable group, while younger patients (18–24) are least represented and more prone to no‑shows.
